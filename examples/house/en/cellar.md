# At the Cellar Door {#listen}

The chanting is Latin, but wrong Latin, the way someone speaks it who
learned it from someone who could not speak it either. Between the lines
falls your name. Your heart skips a beat. Then dinner comes back to you: the
questions, the attentive listening, the nodding.

* Back to the stairs

## The Butler Comes {#servant-fight}

You walk in, right in, take the coat off the chair and put it on. Not one
robe stirs. No one stops you. It is the eeriest thing this house has done to
you yet.

One after another, eleven figures sit down, at first with dignity, then
quickly.

It grows so hot you nearly drop it, and the chanting stops.

The ones who want to stop you would first have to stand up. They try. It
looks like a bad gym lesson.

Twelve heads turn at once, which makes more of an impression than anything
so far.

## The Guards {#guards}

Two robes detach themselves from the circle and step into your way, with the

* Back to the door

better.

# Alone {#alone}

The master of the house stands at the end between eleven snoring robes, and

"Good staff," he says, "has always been the problem out here."

# The Name {#named}

You speak it. It is not hard to pronounce, which is the most astonishing
thing about it.

The chanting stops. The master goes very still. "From where," he says, and
gets no further. Something leaves him - posture, colour, size, in that
order. Names are contracts, you read somewhere, presumably tonight. His has
just been terminated.

You try to think the name once more. It is gone. Spent like a match.

## The Twelfth Jar {#jar}

It is faster than anything that size has a right to be. The floor comes up
to meet you, the candles drift towards the ceiling, and your last thought is
of disarming clarity: in the pantry upstairs stands an empty jar with a
fresh label, and someone is going to write your name on it very neatly.

Your adventure ends here.

# The Circle Breaks {#break}

Whatever was patiently assembled here over years collapses, and literally
so: the candles, the robes, the chair, the house.

You walk out through a door that ten seconds later no longer exists.

Outside it is morning. Your car stands on the road and starts at the first
try, which you consider the single most incredible event of the night.

{knows("HUND-FREUND"): The hound is already sitting in the passenger seat. Strictly speaking he is not your dog, but strictly speaking it is no longer his house.}

# The Front Door {#flight}

You walk out, down the gravel, through the gate, which stands open as if it
had never been otherwise.

Two miles on, a milk lorry comes towards you. The driver takes you along,

# Not Survived {#undone}

It is not the creature, not the butler, not the hound. It is the sum. Your
heart has worked harder tonight than in all the years before, and at some
point it works no more.

You have lost more in this house than can be replaced, and at some point it
was enough.

Your adventure ends here.
