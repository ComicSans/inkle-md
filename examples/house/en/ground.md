clock against the wall strikes once, although its face claims three o'clock.

# The Grandfather Clock {#clock}

The pendulum swings, but wrongly: at the top left it hesitates every time,
like someone drawing breath in the middle of a lie. A draught comes from
behind the clock case.

Twenty years on the local desk: you read diagonally, back to front, and the
story sits where it always sits, between the columns. Arrival dates, page

## Caught {#caught}

The little bell rings out into a house where nobody is left to hear it. The
only one who would have come is lying at the cellar door. You wait a whole
minute anyway before you turn back round.

The butler stands in the doorway, a candlestick in one hand and something
elongated in the other. "The study," he says, "is among the rooms
particularly dear to the master."

* Out, and slam the door behind you

You leave him standing there, and your politeness with him.
