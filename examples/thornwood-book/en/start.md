# At the Forest Edge {#begin}

The path forks before a thorn hedge. {&A crack|A crunch|Silence} in the undergrowth.

You still have {gold} gold pieces and a luck of {luck}.

* Left, into the thicket
* Right, towards the brook
* Into the gap under the hedge

You squeeze in, lantern first.

# In the Thicket {#thicket}

{!Thorns rake across your arms.|You know the way through the thorns by now.}

* On, until the wood thins out
* Back to the hedge

# At the Brook {#brook}

The water is clear enough to show the bottom. Something pale lies in it.

* Reach for it
* Jump the brook
* Back to the hedge

Your fingers close around a coin.

You land safely on the far side.

The brook runs on, indifferent to you.
