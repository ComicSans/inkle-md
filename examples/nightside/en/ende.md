# Out of Air {#erstickt}

{ air <= 0 }
  The gauge reads zero. It has not lied all night, and it is not going to start now.
{ else }
  The gauge still shows a remainder. It is not the air, it is the body, and the body does not count in minutes.

You sit down. It is not a decision, it is only the next step, and it does not come.

{knows("GESTAENDNIS"): ARIS keeps talking, calm and in complete sentences. From now on she carries your channel too.}

Your adventure ends here.

-> END

# The Orbit Window {#rettung}

The beacon has been transmitting all night, and now someone answers it. The window opens, the capsule comes down, and it has exactly one seat.

{knows("GESTAENDNIS"): ARIS reports six survivors to the rescue crew. You do not correct it right away. You do not correct it at all.}
{knows("GRAB"): Below you the hollow shrinks until it looks like landscape again.}

Up there they will ask what happened on the nightside. You have the whole climb to settle on a version, and you already know which parts will be missing.

-> END

# Staying {#bleiben}

You switch off the radio. It is not a grand gesture, just a switch. The filter is running.

{knows("ANLAGE"): The machinery in the deepest chamber breathes, and you breathe with it.|Somewhere behind the wall something you have never seen is working, and you breathe with it.}

{knows("AUFGEHOERT"): The others here stopped while there was still enough left. You clear yourself a corner anyway, far enough from them to be polite.}

Someday someone will find this chamber: suits, all facing inward, and one of them a size too small.

-> END

# Shutting ARIS Down {#abschalten}

You open the maintenance hatch and disconnect ARIS. It is two contacts, and it takes less time than it should.

The silence afterwards is complete, for about four seconds.

{knows("KENNUNG"): Then the tone comes back: three, then seven, then three again.|Then something on the channel says your name, once, with the right stress.}

You do not answer, and you do not close the channel.

-> END

# The Morning {#dunkel}

You decided on nothing, and the morning did not wait for you. The light comes in flat over the ridge, grey at first, then in a colour you brought no word for.

{knows("GESTAENDNIS"): ARIS says nothing more. She stopped when you stopped asking.|ARIS reports the crew assembling at the wreck field. You do not go.}

The suit works out for you how long the air will last. You turn the readout out of your field of view and watch the morning.

-> END
